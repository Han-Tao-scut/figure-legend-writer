# Official guidance and verification ledger

Snapshot updated: **2026-10-15** for Nature, Nature Communications, Science and PLOS Biology figure guidance. Cell and PLOS submission-placement notes retain the **2026-10-14** access result. Recheck at use time. These are legend-specific summaries, not full submission instructions; absence here does not establish absence of a journal rule.

## Nature (flagship)

- [Initial submission](https://www.nature.com/nature/for-authors/initial-submission), Figures/Statistical information: live official text read on 2026-10-15. Legends **<250 words**, brief title and description of what is depicted, sufficient for independent understanding; present with figure. Report exact n, replicate definitions, error bars, repeat counts for representative results and relevant exact P values; t-test/ANOVA statistics and degrees of freedom where applicable.
- [Formatting guide](https://www.nature.com/nature/for-authors/formatting-guide), Figure legends: live page read. Legends **<300 words**, title, panels and symbols; define error bars/statistics. Detailed procedures belong in Methods. Final legends in text after main references, separate from figure files.
- Different pages give different limits. Apply initial-guide <250 for initial submission; do not claim a universal Nature cap. If stage unknown, <250 is a provisional conservative writing target; confirm type/stage/editor requirements.

## Nature Communications

[How to submit](https://www.nature.com/ncomms/submit/how-to-submit), Figure legends/General figure guidelines: live official text read on 2026-10-15. **≤350 words**, title, panel descriptions, symbols and minimal methodology; numerical order after references. Define unusual units/abbreviations and scale-bar lengths; lower-case panel labels. Recheck linked field-specific reporting policies. Do not import limits from flagship Nature or other Nature Portfolio journals.

## Science (flagship)

Official [initial manuscript instructions](https://www.science.org/content/page/instructions-preparing-initial-manuscript), Figure captions/Preparation of figures, and [revised manuscript instructions](https://www.science.org/content/page/instructions-preparing-revised-manuscript) were read in the live browser on **2026-10-15** after earlier retrieval failures. Captions: **no more than 200 words**, single-spaced and numerically ordered; begin with a bold, brief whole-figure title without panel/reference citations. Describe uppercase panels in order; terminology/symbols/units must match the text. Report N, P and the actual test in the appropriate caption or main text. Initial figures are embedded in the manuscript; revision uploads separate figures with legends retained in the text. These are verified current snapshot rules, to be checked again at use time. Science Advances and other Science-family journals are not substitutes.

## PLOS Biology

- [Figures](https://journals.plos.org/plosbiology/s/figures), Captions: live page read. Figure label and title required, title **≤15 words**; legend follows title, credits last. Legends are formally optional on this page; for the requested substantive legend explain panels and nonstandard symbols/abbreviations and keep it independently understandable. No whole-legend word cap identified in the reviewed section; recheck instead of asserting no limit exists.
- [Submission guidelines](https://journals.plos.org/plosbiology/s/submission-guidelines), Figures: live page read. Captions after first citation paragraph in numeric order. Separate uploads specified here; Figures page also offers combined PDF for initial/full submission. Resolve stage-specific instructions if asked about file submission.

## Verification record

Report journal/type/stage, official URL, access date, section, rule, status (live text / indexed snippet / inaccessible), applicability and conflicts. Search official domains nature.com, science.org, cell.com, journals.plos.org; follow current author-portal links and redirects. Never present snippet-only access as live full-text verification. Applicable current rules outrank snapshots; permissive writing style cannot make unsupported study claims valid.
