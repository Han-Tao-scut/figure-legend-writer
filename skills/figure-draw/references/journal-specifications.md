Do not treat 5 pt as an optimal target: use 7 pt when space allows. The 8 pt panel-label exception is explicit in Specifications even though the general text limit is 7 pt. Raster guidance also differs by page/section: initial page ≥300 dpi, Specifications export section ≥450 dpi for images, Extended Data page maximum 300 dpi. Apply the relevant content/stage rule, not a universal DPI. Vector line art has no intrinsic DPI; rasterized components do. Check the current production letter if rules conflict. Nature's historical [Final guide PDF](https://www.nature.com/documents/Final_guide_to_authors.pdf) was read; use current HTML for conflicts.

## Science flagship — official HTML and PDF read on 2026-10-15

The live [initial guide](https://www.science.org/content/page/instructions-preparing-initial-manuscript), Figure captions/Preparation of figures, specifies ≤200-word captions; 10 pt bold capital panels; Helvetica-preferred sans serif; symbols ≥6 pt, lines ≥0.5 pt; no minor ticks/grids; quantities with parenthetical units; sentence-case labels, leading zeros and italic variables. Keep captions/titles outside artwork. N/P/test reporting belongs in the appropriate caption or main text; do not fabricate them.

The live [revised guide](https://www.science.org/content/page/instructions-preparing-revised-manuscript), Preparing revised figures, gives 57/121/184 mm widths, lettering about 7 pt (minimum 5), vectors preferred and ≥300 dpi rasters at final size without upsampling. Submit separate PDF/EPS/AI vectors or TIFF rasters at revision; embedded Word/PowerPoint figures are rejected.

The directly linked [2025 figure PDF](https://www.science.org/cms/asset/67f37ac8-4d02-4625-8a05-230568cb8323/author_prep_guide_2025.pdf) was downloaded and all three pages rendered/read. Page 1 specifies Arial/Helvetica, 6–9 pt labels, 10 pt bold capitals, editable layers/text, RGB, embedded images and no outlining/flattening. Page 2 depicts 90/183 mm widths. The PDF minimum line weight is 0.28 pt, whereas HTML says 0.5 pt. These official differences remain visible: use journal-specific HTML widths and ≥0.5 pt lines unless editor instructions resolve otherwise. A 7 pt body satisfies both sources. PDF raster options are broader than revision HTML; apply stage-specific HTML upload formats. The former university mirror is no longer used to establish current policy.

Helper settings: explicit width, 7 pt body, 10 pt panels, ≥6 pt line markers, 0.6 pt axes, 0.9 pt data lines, editable PDF/SVG. SVG remains an editable source; use PDF/EPS/AI for the specified revision upload. Local choices are not a universal compliance certificate.

## Additional evidence for statistical choices — recommendations, not journal mandates

- Weissgerber TL, Milic NM, Winham SJ, Garovic VD. **Beyond Bar and Line Graphs: Time for a New Data Presentation Paradigm**. PLOS Biology, 2015-04-22. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.1002128). Summary-only bars can conceal continuous-data distributions and paired designs; show observations/pairing where available.
- Crameri F, Shephard GE, Heron PJ. **The misuse of colour in science communication**. Nature Communications, 2020-10-28. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-020-19160-7). Match perceptually suitable sequential/diverging/cyclic colour maps to data and include the colour scale. This is visualization evidence, not Nature Communications rules applied to Nature.
- Krzywinski M, Altman N. **Visualizing samples with box plots**. Nature Methods, 2014-02. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/nmeth.2813). Citation metadata verified; article body was not accessible in the retrieved HTML. Do not attribute detailed recommendations to unread text.

Record for every exported figure: journal/type/stage; policy URL/date/section; verified and unresolved rules; local defaults; actual dimensions/fonts; data/statistical evidence and visual-QA status. Never label a draft “Nature/Science compliant” solely because a preset was applied.
